# About me

Hi, I’m **Arataki Asahi** (荒泷旭). You can call me **Nero**.

🎮 I like playing games and figuring out how they’re made. *Ghost of Tsushima* and *Red Dead Redemption 2* are two of my favorites. I also collect anime and game merch.

I use Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, English, and Spanish. Feel free to say hi in any of them!
